usage: dataset_summary.py [-h] --manifest MANIFEST [--out OUT]
                          [--write-filtered] [--env ENV] [--pin PIN]

Record reproduction metadata from builder manifests, without running a model.
The builder's fail-before/pass-after records are retained, including
unsuccessful attempts. This summarizes those records; it does not claim to
rerun their tests.

options:
  -h, --help           show this help message and exit
  --manifest MANIFEST
  --out OUT
  --write-filtered
  --env ENV            NAME=PYTHON (record package versions)
  --pin PIN            NAME=GIT_CHECKOUT (record source revision)
